name: Werewolf
type: Party
players: 7-20
minutes: 45
---

1. The gist
Hidden roles and table talk: a village tries to find the wolves among them before the wolves pick everyone off.

2. Players and time


3. Materials and links
Role cards (or slips of paper), a moderator

4. Setup
The moderator secretly hands out roles: 2 werewolves for ~8 players, one seer, one doctor, the rest villagers.

5. How to play
Night: everyone closes their eyes. Werewolves wake and silently choose a victim. The seer wakes and learns one player's role. The doctor wakes and protects one player. Day: the moderator reveals who died. Everyone argues and votes to eliminate one suspect. Villagers win when all wolves are out; wolves win when they equal the villagers.

6. Variations
Add a Hunter, Witch, or Cupid. One Night Ultimate Werewolf is a 10-minute version.

7. Rulemaster
Andrew Plotkin, based on Mafia by Dimitry Davidoff (1986)
