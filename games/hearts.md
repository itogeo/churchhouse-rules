1. The gist
Trick-taking where you avoid points: every heart costs 1, the Queen of Spades costs 13. Or take them all and shoot the moon.

2. Players and time
Best with 4

3. Materials and links
One standard 52-card deck, paper to keep score

4. Setup
Deal all 52 cards (13 each). Each player passes 3 cards: left, then right, then across, then no pass, repeating.

5. How to play
Whoever holds the 2 of clubs leads it. Follow suit if you can; if you can't, play anything. Highest card of the led suit wins the trick and leads next. Hearts can't be led until one has been played (broken). After all tricks, add up points. If one player takes every heart plus the Queen of Spades (shooting the moon), they score 0 and everyone else takes 26. Game ends when someone passes 100; lowest score wins.

6. Variations
Jack of Diamonds counts -10. No points allowed on the first trick.

7. Rulemaster
Traditional
