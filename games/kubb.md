1. The gist
Viking chess: knock down the other team's wooden blocks with thrown batons, then topple the king.

2. Players and time


3. Materials and links
Kubb set: 10 kubbs, 1 king, 6 batons, 4 corner stakes

4. Setup
Mark a pitch about 5 x 8 m. Stand 5 kubbs on each baseline and the king in the center.

5. How to play
Teams alternate throwing all six batons underhand, end over end, at the other side's kubbs. Kubbs you knock down get thrown back onto their team's half and stood up where they land (field kubbs); those must be knocked down before baseline kubbs. Once a team has cleared every kubb on the other side, it can throw at the king. Knock the king down to win. Knock it down early and you lose.
