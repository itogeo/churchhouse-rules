1. The gist
Bet drinks on a suit, then watch four aces race down a track of cards.

2. Players and time


3. Materials and links
One standard deck, drinks

4. Setup
Pull the four aces and line them up side by side at the start. Lay 6-8 cards face down in a column beside them as the track. Everyone bets a number of drinks on one suit.

5. How to play
The dealer flips cards off the deck one at a time. Each flip moves the ace of that suit forward one step. When every ace has passed a track card, flip that track card; its suit moves back one step. The first ace past the end of the track wins. Everyone who bet on it hands out their bet; everyone else drinks theirs.

6. Variations
Winners hand out double.

7. Rulemaster
Traditional
