---
name: Murder in the Dark
type: Indoor
players: 6-15
minutes: 20
---

1. The gist
Lights off, one secret murderer, one detective: a whodunit you play with your whole body in a dark house.

2. Players and time
More rooms and more people make it better

3. Materials and links
A dark room or house, a deck of cards

4. Setup
Pull one card per player, including the Ace of Spades (murderer) and a King (detective). Everyone draws secretly. The detective reveals themselves and leaves the room.

5. How to play
Lights go out and everyone wanders. The murderer finds a victim and taps them. The victim counts to 3 and screams, then drops. Lights on, detective comes in. The detective questions everyone; everyone except the murderer must tell the truth. The detective gets one accusation. Right, detective wins; wrong, the murderer wins.
