1. The gist
Shedding game with a social ladder: finish order sets the ranks, and the President gets the Scum's best cards next round.

2. Players and time


3. Materials and links
One standard deck (two for big groups)

4. Setup
Deal out the whole deck. After the first round, the Scum hands their 2 best cards to the President, who hands back any 2.

5. How to play
Starting player leads a single, pair, triple or quad. Each next player must beat it with the same number of cards of higher rank, or pass. 2s are high. When everyone passes, the last player to play clears the pile and leads. First out becomes President, then Vice President, and so on. Last out is Scum.

6. Variations
A 2 clears the pile instantly. Playing the same rank skips the next player. Often played as a drinking game.

7. Rulemaster
Traditional (also called Asshole or Scum)
