1. The gist
Memory game: you're trying to hold the lowest total in four face-down cards you've only half seen.

2. Players and time


3. Materials and links
Cabo deck (or a standard deck with face cards valued per house rules)

4. Setup
Deal 4 cards face down in a row to each player. Everyone peeks at their two nearest cards once.

5. How to play
On your turn draw from the deck or take the top discard. Swap it for one of your cards or discard it. Discarding certain cards triggers powers: 7-8 peek at your own card, 9-10 spy on someone else's, J-Q blind swap with someone. Call "Cabo" at the start of your turn instead of drawing; everyone else gets one last turn, then reveal. Lowest total scores 0 for the round; everyone else scores their total. Out at 100.

6. Variations
Kamikaze: two 12s and two 13s in hand scores 0 and everyone else takes 50.
