1. The gist
Spelling game with no board: add letters to a growing word and don't be the one to finish it.

2. Players and time
2+

3. Materials and links
Nothing

4. Setup
Pick an order.

5. How to play
Each player adds one letter to the fragment. The fragment must always be the start of a real word. If you complete a word of 4+ letters, you get a letter: G, then H, and so on. If you think the last player added a letter with no real word behind it, challenge them. If they can't name a word, they take the letter; if they can, you do. Spell GHOST and you're out.

6. Variations
Superghost: letters can be added to either end.

7. Rulemaster
Traditional
