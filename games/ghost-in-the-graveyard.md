---
name: Ghost in the Graveyard
type: Outdoor
players: 5-20
minutes: 30
---

1. The gist
Hide and seek run backwards: one ghost hides, everyone else hunts, and whoever spots the ghost sprints home before being tagged.

2. Players and time
5+

3. Materials and links
A dark yard and a home base

4. Setup
Pick a ghost and a base. The ghost hides while everyone else counts at base.

5. How to play
The group counts out loud together: "One o'clock, two o'clock, three o'clock... midnight! Starlight, star bright, hope to see a ghost tonight!" Then everyone searches. Whoever spots the ghost yells "Ghost in the graveyard!" and everyone runs for base. Anyone the ghost tags becomes a ghost next round.

6. Variations
Tagged players join the ghost team for the rest of the round.

7. Rulemaster
Traditional
