---
name: Mafia
type: Party
players: 7-20
minutes: 45
---

1. The gist
The original social deduction game: an informed minority kills at night while the town votes to hang someone every day.

2. Players and time
Needs a moderator who doesn't play

3. Materials and links
Playing cards or slips of paper for roles, a moderator

4. Setup
The moderator secretly deals roles: about one mafia per four players, one detective, one doctor, the rest townspeople.

5. How to play
Night: everyone closes their eyes. The mafia wake, recognize each other, and silently pick a victim. The detective wakes and points at someone; the moderator nods if they're mafia. The doctor wakes and saves one person. Day: the moderator tells the story of who died. The town discusses and votes to eliminate one player, who reveals their role. Town wins when all mafia are gone; mafia wins when they equal the town.

6. Variations
Werewolf is the same game with werewolves.

7. Rulemaster
Dimitry Davidoff (Moscow State University, 1986)
