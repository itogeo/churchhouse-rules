type: Dice
players: 2-6

1. The gist
Bluffing with hidden dice: bid on how many of a face are on the whole table, or call the last bidder a liar.

2. Players and time


3. Materials and links
5 dice and a cup per player

4. Setup
Everyone shakes and looks at their own dice without showing anyone.

5. How to play
Players take turns raising the bid: a quantity and a face, e.g. "four 3s." Each bid must raise the quantity, the face, or both. Instead of bidding, call the previous bid a lie. All dice are revealed. If the bid was true, the caller loses a die; if false, the bidder does. Last player holding dice wins.

6. Variations
1s are wild. Spot on: call the bid exactly right and everyone else loses a die.

7. Rulemaster
Traditional (Perudo in South America)
