name: Flip Cup Elimination
type: Drinking
players: 4-20
minutes: 20
---

1. The gist
Flip cup with no teams: every round the slowest flipper is out, until one champion is left.

2. Players and time


3. Materials and links
Cups, a table, drinks

4. Setup
Everyone stands around the table with a cup a little bit full, upright at the table's edge.

5. How to play
On go, everyone drinks, sets the cup lip-down over the edge, and flicks it until it lands upside down. The last person to land it is out. Keep going until one person is left.

6. Variations
Classic flip cup: two teams race in a relay down the table.

7. Rulemaster
Traditional
