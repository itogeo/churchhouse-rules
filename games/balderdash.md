1. The gist
Bluff: invent a fake definition for an obscure word good enough that your friends pick it over the real one.

2. Players and time


3. Materials and links
Balderdash box, or any big dictionary, plus paper and pens

4. Setup
Pick a reader for the round.

5. How to play
The reader announces an obscure word. Everyone writes a fake definition; the reader copies the real one. The reader shuffles and reads them all aloud, and everyone votes for the one they think is real. Score 2 for picking the real one, 1 for every vote your fake gets. The reader scores 3 if nobody finds the real one. Pass the reader role left.

6. Variations
Fictionary: the same game with just a dictionary.

7. Rulemaster
Laura Robinson and Paul Toyne (1984), based on the parlor game Fictionary
