1. The gist
Two teams, one bottle in the middle, and you only drink while the other team is scrambling.

2. Players and time
Also spelled Flukiball

3. Materials and links
A ball, a plastic bottle part-filled with water, one drink per player, open ground

4. Setup
Two teams line up facing each other about 10 m apart, each player with a full drink at their feet. Stand the bottle halfway between.

5. How to play
Teams take turns throwing the ball at the bottle. On a hit, the throwing team drinks while the other team runs out, fetches the ball, stands the bottle back up and gets back behind their line, then yells stop. First team to finish every drink wins.
