1. The gist
Every card in the deck is a rule, and the fourth King drinks the middle cup.

2. Players and time


3. Materials and links
One standard deck, a big cup, drinks

4. Setup
Spread the cards face down in a ring around the empty King's Cup.

5. How to play
Take turns drawing. Common rules: A waterfall, 2 you (pick someone to drink), 3 me, 4 floor, 5 guys, 6 chicks, 7 heaven, 8 mate, 9 rhyme, 10 categories, J make a rule, Q questions, K pour some into the cup. Whoever draws the fourth King drinks the cup.

6. Variations
Break the ring and you drink.

7. Rulemaster
Traditional
